Fiberglass Doors by Fibercraft

West Windows has dealt with many door supplier companies and has found one that rises above the competition, “Fibercraft Door Company.” Fibercraft is located in the small town of Hanover, Ontario. Hanover was a furniture mecca in the 1800’s and is one of the few companies that carry on the tradition today.

Styles: Traditional, Stained Traditional (oak, mahogany, cherry, douglas fir), Modern, and Rail & Stile up to 48" × 108".
